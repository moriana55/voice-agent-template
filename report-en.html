01 · What it does

Answers, converses, and captures the lead

The system does what a front-desk agent does: greets the caller, understands the question, answers it, and by the end of the call has turned the conversation into usable information — who called, their number, what they want, and when they want to be called back.

StepWhat happensTime
1. ListeningSilence is detected while the caller speaks; the recording is sent automatically. If the caller interrupts, the agent stops talking.—
2. UnderstandingSpeech is transcribed (Fish Audio transcribe-1).instant
3. ThinkingClaude generates the reply; call state (name / phone / appointment captured?) updates continuously.~6 ms first word
4. SpeakingFish Audio S2 Pro streams the voice in chunks; playback starts before the sentence is finished.~1.1 s
5. RecordingThe completed request is written to storage and can be pushed to CRM and calendar.—
Why “time to first speech” is the metric that matters: on a phone call, silence is what makes people hang up. The agent does not wait for the full answer — it starts speaking as soon as the first syllable is ready. The measured 1.1 seconds is roughly the pause a human takes before answering.

How the conversation actually goes

Transcript from the live test — unedited:

Caller
Hi, I'm interested in buying land. Can you help me?
Nova
Great! I'd be happy to help you explore land options. What's the best name to have on file for you?
Caller
Do you offer owner financing? What's the down payment?
Nova
Great question — I'd love to connect you with someone who can walk through our financing options in detail. What's the best number to reach you?
Caller
My name is John Miller, my number is 555 340 1122, please call me back.
Nova
Great, John — what day and time would work best for you to receive a callback?

Two things worth noting. First, the agent does not ramble — it asks for one missing field at a time and never re-asks something it already has. Second, when it hits a question it should not answer on its own (financing terms), it hands off to a human instead of inventing an answer. That behaviour is enforced by design, not by luck.

03 · What you get out of it

The output is not a recording — it's a filled-in lead

When the call ends, what remains is a structured record. On screen it fills in live as the conversation progresses:

FieldHow it gets filled
NameCaptured the moment the caller says it
PhoneSpoken digits transcribed into a number
Request typeIntent classified (purchase, information, appointment…)
Date and timeNatural phrasing understood (“tomorrow afternoon”)
SummaryOne-line summary of the call

Completed records can be pushed to CRM and calendar via webhooks — the appointment can be on the calendar before the caller has put the phone down.

It can take real phone calls

The system is not limited to a browser. A real phone number can be connected through Twilio: the number rings, the agent answers, speaks, and creates the record. All that is needed is a number and a public domain.

Security and privacy

  • API keys stay on the server; they are never sent to the browser.
  • AES-256 encryption support for stored records, with a default 30-day retention.
  • Explicit consent is collected before the conversation starts.
  • IP-based rate limiting, upload size/type limits, same-origin protection.
  • Structured logs that carry no personal data.
Before going live: record encryption is currently off (trial environment). If real customer calls will be stored, an encryption key must be configured. CRM and calendar webhooks are built but not yet connected to a target system.

Where it fits

  • Inbound land enquiries: answers calls from listings, captures who is asking and which parcel they are interested in.
